    The image or fact isn't loading

    Star Log runs on NASA's shared demo API key, which gets rate-limited when a lot of people are using it at once. Wait a moment and turn the page again — if it still won't load after a few tries, the archive itself may be briefly down.

    Some entries show a placeholder instead of a photo

    Occasionally NASA's picture of the day is a video rather than a still image. When that happens, Star Log shows a thumbnail if one's available, or a simple placeholder if not.

    Can I pick a specific date instead of a random one?

    Yes — use the date field under the "Turn the page" button to jump straight to any day since June 16, 1995, when APOD began.

    How do saved entries work?

    Tap "Save this entry" on any page you like, and it's added to the Saved list above. Saved entries are stored in your browser only — they're not sent anywhere, and clicking a saved title jumps straight back to that day.
