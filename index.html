About Star Log

A science exploration project for curious minds.

Star Log brings public astronomy resources into one readable, mobile-friendly interface. Discoveries are fetched from NASA's public endpoints when online. The app shell is cached for offline access, although new images and live data require an internet connection.

Data note: NASA's Astronomy Picture of the Day may include work from different observatories and photographers. A NASA-hosted image is not automatically a Hubble observation; check each item's description and source for details.
